Full bio

KeyNee is an independent electronic music artist, both a music producer and DJ, from Southern Arizona, making melodic bass music and future bass with an emphasis on emotional authenticity. KeyNee's sound is both soft and hard, with a focus on creating music that feels like something, rather than music that sounds like something.

KeyNee's sound is an amalgamation of cinematic, glitch, and bass music, with all songs independently produced, written, mixed, and mastered. Releases such as Echo Chamber and Clouds mark KeyNee's path in the electronic music scene with a futuristic look and music that feels real.
